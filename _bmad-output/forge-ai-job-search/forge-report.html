AI Job Search

A local web app for a jobless, zero-budget, Philippines-based developer to find eligible remote roles early and send a CV tailored to each one. The measure of success is responses from recruiters.

Locked decisions

  • Core pain is being late, not having too few options. By the time a posting is seen, 100+ people have applied. The app optimizes for being early.
  • Sources: Remotive, RemoteOK, We Work Remotely, Himalayas, Jobicy, OnlineJobs.ph, Jobstreet, Kalibrr. Public APIs and RSS feeds come before HTML scraping, and each source still needs checking.
  • A background fetcher runs every 30 minutes into local MongoDB. The page reads from Mongo and never fetches live.
  • Eligibility comes first. Keyword rules decide whether a Philippines-based applicant can take the job. Only eligible roles are shown. Unknowns are shown with a badge, and restricted roles are hidden.
  • Ranking: freshness tiers (<24h, 1–3d, older), sorted by CV skill match within each tier. The minimum match is a setting, 40% by default.
  • Resume: upload your PDF once. Claude turns it into a master resume and you review it. Claude tailors it for each job, and the app renders a PDF in its own ATS-friendly template.
  • Tailoring never invents anything. It only reorders and rewords true facts.
  • Claude runs through claude -p on your subscription and is used for resume work only.

Rejected, and why

  • LinkedIn and Indeed as the main source. It's the same feed the crowd sees, it breaks easily, and scraping it breaks their terms of service.
  • Claude for scraping, eligibility or ranking. Claude is reserved for resume work.
  • A blended match×age score. Tiers make it clearer why a job ranked where it did.
  • Keeping the original CV design. Getting responses matters more than design.
  • Notifications in v1. Deferred by your choice.

Weak points that survived

  • Build time competes with time spent applying. Time-box v1 to about two weeks and keep applying by hand in parallel.
  • Subscription usage limits cap how many CVs you can tailor.
  • Rules-only eligibility means a large "unknown" bucket.
  • Without alerts, being early depends on opening the app often.

Parked for after v1

  • Application tracking (statuses, which CV version was sent) and response-rate stats by source, match band and how fast you applied.
  • Telegram alerts for high-match jobs posted in the last 24h.
  • Local embeddings if dictionary matching turns out to be too blunt.
